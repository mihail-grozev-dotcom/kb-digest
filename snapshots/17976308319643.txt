Title: Archived Surveys Report
The archived surveys report displays surveys that have been archived. You can unarchive surveys on the Archived Surveys page.
Note: Only company Supervisor or Staff roles can access the archived surveys report from the company page.
1: Accessing the Archived Surveys Report
In the Portal, open your company page by clicking your company name or logo. Then click the Archived Surveys in the left panel of the page.
2: Archived Surveys Report Overview
1. Filter by Survey Name: Search by survey name.
2. Name: The name of the archived survey.
3. Path: The url path for the archived survey.
4. Archived The date and time the survey was archived. Time is based on the server's time.
5. Clear Data: The date and time that participant data is cleared. This is based on a configurable company setting. If no date displays, participant data is not cleared prior to the Delete date. See Create and Manage Companies to learn more.
6. Delete: The date and time the survey will be deleted. The server default is 365 days.
7. Unarchive: To restore a survey, click Unarchive.
8. Pagination: Ten archived surveys are displayed per page. To jump to a specific page, use the text box at right, then click Go.
