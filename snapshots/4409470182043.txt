Title: Sending Emails in the Campaign Manager
Emails can only be sent to a recipient list once the following steps have been completed. You may click the links below to review the documentation on how to accomplish each of these steps.
An email campaign has been created.
The seed list has been created and test emails have been sent and reviewed.
The survey has been set "live" (i.e., launched).
The recipient list has been uploaded.
1: Launching a Campaign
Once you have completed the steps above and you are ready to launch your campaign, click "Launch Campaign" to begin the launch process. Then select "Send Emails".
1.1: Reviewing the Campaign Information
A new window appears, displaying the campaign name and recipient list file, including the number of recipients in the list. Review this information before proceeding with the send.
1.2: Configuring Send Settings
Next, make your selections for shuffling, including the seed list and making the list unabridged.
Shuffle List: By default, the order of the recipient list will be randomized (i.e., it will send to recipients in random order). To send the emails in order, uncheck the "Shuffle List" check box.
Include Seed List: By default, the seed list is included in the email send. Uncheck the box if you do not want to include the seed list in the email send.
Unabridged List: By default, the system will not send to email addresses it identifies as duplicates. If you want to recontact a recipient in a multi-wave survey, check the box to make the list unabridged (i.e., allow repeat email sends to an email address).
Tip: Do not make a list unabridged in a soft send. A duplicate may appear in the first send that would not be sent to in the second send. In such a case, split the list manually.
Note: For surveys using the ADB feature (ADB=1), survey links in Campaign Manager seed email sends will only open the survey if those recipients can test the survey (that is, the recipient is logged in, or the survey is in testing mode).
1.3: Filtering by Variable (Optional)
The Campaign Manager gives you the option to filter your recipient list by a variable, allowing you to send emails to a specific group. This step is optional. Click "Add Recipient List Filter".
Select the variable from the drop-down menu. Then enter how you want to filter the chosen variable. To further restrict this filter, click "+AND" and add additional restrictions. To add a second, independent filter, click "Add Filter" and follow the same steps. Once finished, click "Apply Filters".
Once applied, you have the option to edit or delete the filter.
1.4: Sending Emails in Batches (Optional)
You have the option to send emails to a specified number of sample. To do this, enter the number for the first batch of emails. This step is optional.
1.5: Scheduling the Send (Optional)
By default, the soft send occurs 20 minutes after "Send Emails" is clicked. If you prefer to set a specific date and time for the email send to go out, expand the "Schedule Delayed Send" section.
Tip: The scheduled send time must be set for at least 20 minutes after the time you click "Send Emails".
Click the calendar field and select the date from the calendar pop-up, then click in the time field to select the exact time of the send and select the time zone from the drop-down menu. The selected date / time appears in the fields as you adjust them. If a field is left empty, the first 10,000 emails will be sent at the default time of 20 minutes and the remainder will be sent after another 20 minutes.
If you are sending emails in batches, a "Send Remaining" field is included, allowing you to specify the number of remaining emails to send and schedule the date and time they will be sent out.
1.6: Sending the Emails
If everything looks good, click "Send Emails" to initiate the email send.
If the email send has not been scheduled for a specific time and date, the emails are sent to the seed list immediately and a soft send will go out after 20 minutes. The "Status" column will display the count-down. This allows time to review the email and cancel the soft send if changes needed. The email send can only be cancelled during the 20 minute count-down, while the send is being prepared. Once the 20 minutes are up the email send cannot be cancelled.
The soft send will go out to the first 10,000 recipients the email campaign. If the entire email campaign contains 10,000 recipients or less, then the entire recipient list will be emailed in the soft send.
The rest of the email send (i.e., full send) will go out 12 hours after the soft send. The status is displayed in the "Status" column of the email campaign.
