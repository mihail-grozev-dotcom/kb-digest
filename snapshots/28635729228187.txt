Title: 153.39 Release Notes
Release Date: August 27, 2024
Release Version: 153.39
New Features
Survey
Survey Editor
In non-Live surveys, when a datasource element is deleted, its corresponding data-upload- and merged- files will now be deleted as well. Note that using Undo, or reverting the XML, will not restore the deleted files.
Bug Fixes
Responses
Response Summary
The counts displayed on the Campaigns tab of Response Summary for New Company Opt-Outs and New Global Opt-Outs now reflect the number of opt-outs that occurred up to 30 days after the initial send. This window was increased from 14 to 30 days in order to capture more new opt-outs. This change is not retroactive for existing campaigns, so older campaigns will continue to reflect the 14 day window. To see all opt-outs, including those that happened more than 30 days after the send, the entire list may be downloaded by using the "Export Opt-Outs" option from the drop down menu on the Campaign Manager List page.
