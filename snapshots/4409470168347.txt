Title: Pausing Quotas in Field
Note: This feature is not available for surveys created using the Survey Editor.
Pausing a quota cell allows you to lock it without changing the limits while the survey is in field. Participants who fall into a paused quota cell will be tagged as overquota.
For an overview of the "Quotas" tab in the Response Summary, see Monitoring Quotas in Field. To learn how to edit quotas, see Editing Quota Limits in Field.
1: Pausing Quotas
To pause a quota cell, first open the Response Summary for the survey and select the "Quotas" tab. Then click "Еdit Quotas".
Each quota cell will appear with a padlock icon. A padlock in the unlocked position indicates the quota is not paused; a padlock in the locked position indicates that the quota is paused. To pause a quota cell, click its associated padlock icon. Then click "Save" to apply your changes.
Once the changes are saved, each paused quota cell will appear with a locked padlock icon and its limit value will be crossed out.
2: Resuming Quotas
To release the pause on a quota cell, follow the same process, again clicking the padlock icon for the paused quota cell and saving the changes. The padlock icon will become unlocked.
