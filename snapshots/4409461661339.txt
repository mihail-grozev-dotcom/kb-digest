Title: About QA Codes
QA codes identify the logic conditions, attributes, and labels for each question in a survey. They make it easier to confirm that questions are correctly configured and displayed.
Additionally, enabling QA codes adds an "answer for me" button you can use to navigate more quickly through a survey. If you want to have random answers populated for you at any question, select the "answer for me" button at the bottom of the question when QA codes are turned "ON". This feature is especially useful when testing large grid questions with multiple answer options.
QA codes are not shown to participants and by default, they are set to OFF in the survey testing toolbar. To view the QA codes for your survey, move the QA codes switch to ON.
To view the QA codes in the crosstab report, click View Options and uncheck Hide QA Codes.

Additionally, the following rules apply to codes for answer attributes:
Codes are written in strikethrough if disabled (i.e., code).
Data values are displayed in bold and square brackets (i.e., [xxxx]).
Question, row, and column labels are displayed in square brackets (i.e., [xxxx]).
