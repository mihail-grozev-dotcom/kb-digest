Title: Monitoring Campaigns in Field
The Campaigns tab is only available if your survey is using Decipher for email sends.
1: Summary Statistics
The first area of the Campaigns tab is the Summary Statistics report. This area lists the statistics for the email campaign including the total number of emails sent, opt-outs, bounce backs, open rate, response rate, etc.
Note: Campaign send statistics cannot be filtered by Date in the Campaigns tab.
1.1: Definition of Statistics
Below is an explanation of how each statistic is calculated in the Summary Statistics report. These definitions can also be viewed by mousing over each item in the report.
Name
Definition
List Total
Total amount of email addresses in all lists
Sent
Number of emails that were attempted to be sent
Errors
Unexpected errors that occurred during the send
Bad/Malformed
Incorrectly formatted email addresses
Duplicate Addresses
Decipher automatically dedupes lists so the recipient will only receive one email
Opt-Out Suppressions
Recipients who opted out prior to the inital send.
New Company Opt-Outs
Recipients added to the company (branded) remove survey up to 30 days after the initial send.
New Global Opt-Outs
Recipients added to the global (unbranded) remove survey up to 30 days after the initial send. Global opt-outs will only apply to your campaign if it is not fully branded.
Throttled Addresses
Recently contacted addresses not included in this send (typically in the last 14 days, however, defaults can be modified if requested)
Bounce-Backs
Email returned from server (address no longer exists)
Blocks
Email rejected by server (potentially temporary error) (for example, when inbox is full)
Additional Suppression
Emails not sent for other reasons (contact already completed the survey, contact previously reported email as spam, email previously bounced, etc.)
Potential Contacts
Number of potential recipients (total sent minus all suppressed emails)
Opened
Measured number of opened invitations
Open Rate
Opened / Potential
Click-Through Rate
Click-Through / Potential
Response Rate
(Terminates + Overquota + Qualified) / Potential
Spam Rate
Spam complaint rate: Spam Complaints / Total Email Sends for all campaigns
See Splitting the Response Summary to learn more.
Unsent
Emails remaining to be sent
1.2: Initial Sends
The next area of the "Campaigns" tab is the Initial Sends report. This area lists the soft sends from your original email list with the date and time in the first column and the total number sent in the second column. If you click on each date and time, a drop-down list will show statistics for that particular send. Click again to hide the statistics.
1.3: Reminders
The final area of the "Campaigns" tab is the Reminders report. This area lists each reminder email sent after the initial email was sent with the date and time in the first column and the total number sent in the second column.
