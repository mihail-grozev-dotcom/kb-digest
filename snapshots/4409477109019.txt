Title: Survey Accessibility and ADA Compliance
Decipher's standard survey questions and themes are optimized for digital accessibility and adhere to at least WCAG 2.2 standards. WCAG standards are the internationally recognized guidelines for creating accessible, ADA-compliant web pages. To meet WCAG 2.2 Level A and AA success criteria (“WCAG 2.2 A/AA”), surveys must be set to a' standard survey questions and themes are optimized for digital accessibility and adhere to WCAG 2.2 standards. WCAG 2.2 standards are the internationally recognized guidelines for creating accessible, ADA-compliant web pages. In order to meet the WCAG 2.2 Level A and AA success criteria (“WCAG 2.2 A/AA”), surveys must be set to a survey compat level of 155+ and
contain only ADA-compliant questions and elements.
In addition to WCAG 2.2 support, Decipher also provides options to help meet Section 508 U.S. accessibility requirements and the EN 301 549 European accessibility standard, allowing you to address accessibility needs across multiple regions.
The "Accessible" theme in the Theme Editor has improved color and contrast to meet accessibility compliance standards. See Editing the Survey Theme to learn how to change your theme.

1: WCAG Compliant Questions and Elements
The following questions and elements are ADA-compliant (or are being updated
to improve compliance). ADA-compliant questions and elements support screen readers,
such as Job Access with Speech (JAWS) or NVDA (NonVisual Desktop Access), as
well as the use of the keyboard to answer questions (e.g., via the Tab button,
space bar, and arrow keys).
