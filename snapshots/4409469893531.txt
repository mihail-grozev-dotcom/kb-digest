Title: End a Survey without Termination
You can use the “final” option in the Survey Editor to end a survey for a participant without termination. The "final" option is similar to showing a participant an exit message, the difference being that the "final" option does not count the participant as a terminate or complete, it simply ends the survey for that participant.
By default, response data for participants that are sent through the "final" option is not saved and not included in the Response Summary.
Note: To save the partial response data for "final" participants, you can enable the Partial Recovery System for your survey.
1: Adding the Option
To end a survey for a participant after they have answered a specific question, click the question so its general options appear to the right of the staging area. Then select "final" from the "more options" drop-down menu and check the box to enable it.
2: Testing the Setup
To test the setup, select "Test Survey" from the "Preview" drop-down in the navigation menu. In the Survey Test Environment, select "Show Participant View" to test the survey as a participant. Once you reach the question you have marked as “final”, select the response options that meet the logic condition you created for that question.
On the following page, you should see the “final” question with a message in a red box explaining that the survey would normally end at that point and the "Continue" button would not display.
Note: This message only appears during testing. Survey participants will see only the "final" question and be unable to continue through the survey
