Title: DQ5 Release Notes
Release date: March 11, 2014
Dynamic Questions: New Question Types
Page Turner (pageturner.1) - Respondents interact with a magazine-like layout, while the system tracks the time spent on each page.
Timer Ad Test (adtest.1) - Respondents view an ad for a predetermined amount of time, and then proceed automatically to the next page.
Slider Points (sliderpoints.1) - Respondents move a slider to a point on the scale to answer the question.
Audio Player (audio.1) - Respondents listen to an audio clip.
