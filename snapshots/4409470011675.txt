Title: Updating References
1: Question and Answer Labels
The Survey Editor performs limited updates when you change a question or answer label that is referenced in element conditions. Label changes will be reflected in "Show If" (cond) and "Answer Piping" (rowCond, colCond, or choiceCond) logic, as well as in quota cell definitions within the survey quota.xls file.
Label references within the following locations will not be updated when labels are changed:
Exec element (exec) logic
Skip element (goto) targets
Answer option logic in Autofill elements (autofill on rows)
"Keep With" option (keepWith) targets
2: Quota Titles
The Survey Editor also performs updates when you change a quota title using logic based on other quotas (hasMarker).
