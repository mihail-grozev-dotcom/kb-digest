Title: M47 Release Notes
Release Date: February 16, 2021
Release Version: 147
Survey Versioning
The new survey compatibility level is 147. The system automatically uses compat="147" for all new surveys. This enables the following:
Enforces secure links for all surveys with a compat level of 147+ (prevents use of secure="0"). Secure surveys require participants to use an HTTPS connection at all times when taking the survey.
Learn more: Survey Compat Levels
New Features
Portal
Company Settings
Added the Archive and Delete Settings feature, which provides staff users with the ability to customize company survey archival and deletion settings.
Learn More: Survey Archival and Deletion
Survey
Survey Editor
Updated the display of the Field Settings menu to include a drop-down of all CNAMEs authorized for use within the company containing the survey.
Architecture/DevTools
Improved system processing when adding new languages after a survey has been created.
Added the hostname of the company server to the default list of valid CNAMEs for each of the company's surveys.
In-App Text Changes
Survey
Survey Editor
Updated the text in the warning window that appears when you click “Save to Live Survey” after making edits to a temp version of a survey.
Learn More: Editing a Live or Closed Survey
Bug Fixes
Portal
Fixed an issue causing errors when attempting to migrate an empty survey from one company to another.
Responses
Sample Marketplace
Fixed an issue preventing the “Get Prices” button from being disabled on click.
Fixed an issue causing an error when attempting to launch a non-secure survey (secure="0") with purchased sample.
Response Summary
Fixed an issue preventing manually disqualified records from being counted in the Terminates tab of the Response Summary.
