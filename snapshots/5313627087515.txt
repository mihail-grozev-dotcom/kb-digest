Title: Managing Salesforce Connections
You can identify your connected Salesforce accounts by clicking
Salesforce under “Integration Settings” on the
Company Page
in Forsta Surveys.
If no connections are listed on this page, visit
Connecting Decipher with Salesforce
to learn how to add a connection.
Note: If you do not see the
Integration Settings > Salesforce option, please contact
your Forsta account representative or
Technical Support.
1: Overview
The Salesforce Integration page includes:
User Name: The username entered for the integration
Description: The description was entered when you
selected the folder
Report Folder Name: The Salesforce name of the selected
folder
Account Type: Either production or sandbox
Connection Status: Active or Inactive
Latest Usage: When the connection was last used
Actions: When you click the ellipsis under “Actions”,
the following options are available for managing each connection:
Edit Details: Allows you to change the description
and choose a different folder.
Test Connection: Verifies that the existing
credentials are working.
Reauthorize: Can be used to reauthorize a
connection when the status of that connection is not indicated by
a green checkmark. Clicking Reauthorize will redirect
you to the Salesforce login screen to provide your account credentials.
Delete Connection: Disables and removes the
connection.
