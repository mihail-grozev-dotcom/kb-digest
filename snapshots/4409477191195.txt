Title: Application Languages
While Decipher is presented in English by default, most of its tools and features are available in a variety of different languages. This document outlines the process for setting your preferred application language.
1: Setting a Preferred Language
If you or a member of your team would prefer to view the Decipher application tools in a language other than English, click “Edit Profile” in the user account menu.
Then select the preferred interface language from the drop-down menu, and click “Save Changes” at the bottom of the profile screen.
Note: Supervisors can also change application languages when creating or editing users in the company portal page.
Once your new language is applied, all menus and information screens for the following tools should appear translated in that language:
Language Manager
Survey Library
Response Summary
Crosstabs
View/Edit Responses
Campaign Manager
CATI Mode
Note: Company and project names will not be affected when translations are applied.
