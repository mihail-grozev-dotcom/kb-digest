Title: Undo / Redo Changes
The Survey Editor allows you to easily undo or redo an action while creating or editing a survey.
1: Undo an Action
To undo a change in your survey, click the counterclockwise arrow icon located at the top left corner of the staging area.
Tip: You can also use your keyboard and click ctrl+z (or cmd+z for Mac devices).
2: Redo an Action
To redo a change in your survey, click the clockwise arrow icon located at the top left corner of the staging area.
Tip: You can also use your keyboard and click ctrl+shift+z (or cmd+shift+z for Mac devices).
