Title: June 30, 2026 Release Notes 
Release Date: The following items were released between June 16, 2026 and June 30, 2026
Bug Fixes
Programming and Tools
It is no longer possible to archive delphi surveys that have child surveys. Any child surveys must be archived first, and then the parent survey may be archived.
Custom Code Elements (Logic Nodes)
AI Compute logic nodes now fully process all queued records even after a survey closes or stops receiving new responses.
