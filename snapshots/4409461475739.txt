Title: Decipher Reserved Words
Decipher reserved words are words or phrases that are used by the system
to perform a specific task or behavior within a survey. When used as a variable
name or label, these words will result in a system error.
1: Reserved Words
Using reserved words as a variable name or label will result in a system error.
Words that are reserved for a compat level higher than your survey should also
be avoided when possible.
