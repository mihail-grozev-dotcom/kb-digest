Imperium RelevantID Integration
Harness the power and security of Imperium's RelevantID system in your Decipher surveys. The new RelevantID integration supports advanced fraud detection, allowing for higher quality data you can trust.
With RelevantID, data gathered from participant computers is put through deterministic algorithms to create a unique fingerprint of each computer. These fingerprints help identify users who have already completed a survey and prevents them from reentry. End survey gaming now by enabling RelevantID on all of your surveys.
